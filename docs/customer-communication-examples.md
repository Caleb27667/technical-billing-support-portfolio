# Customer Communication Examples

These are fictional templates. Adapt them to the verified facts of the individual case.

## Invoice amount clarification

Hello,

Thanks for flagging the invoice. I reviewed the invoice line items and the usage period associated with it. The total is made up of the recurring subscription charge, recorded usage, and the listed adjustments.

I have included the relevant breakdown below so you can see how the total was calculated. If you believe a particular usage item is incorrect, please share its line-item description or ID rather than any payment-card details, and I can investigate that record further.

## Payment still processing

Hello,

I checked the payment status and it is currently showing as pending rather than confirmed as successful or failed. A pending status does not necessarily mean the payment has been completed.

To avoid an accidental duplicate charge, please do not submit another payment while this attempt is unresolved. I will verify the final status and update you once there is a confirmed result.

## Payment failed — customer action needed

Hello,

The latest payment attempt was declined because the payment provider requires an update or additional authentication. Please review the payment method through the secure billing page and complete any requested verification.

For your security, please do not send card numbers, security codes, passwords, or one-time codes in this support conversation.

## Escalation acknowledgement

Hello,

Thank you for your patience. The billing records do not currently agree, so I have escalated the case with the invoice reference, relevant payment attempt, timestamps, and the reconciliation difference.

I do not want to give you an unverified answer. I will share an update once the underlying records have been reviewed.
